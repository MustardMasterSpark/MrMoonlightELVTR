## Scene 01 — Waking At The Campsite

**TRACEY**  *(groggy, hangover)*

## Scene 02 — The Missing Boots

*Outside, minutes later. Her boots are gone from where she left them. She is standing in wet socks on cold ground working out that this is now her whole day. Play the indignity, not the danger. She thinks a friend did this as a joke.*

**TRACEY**

**TRACEY**

**TRACEY**

**TRACEY**

## Scene 04 — Will'S Tent And The Compass

*Inside William's tent, uninvited. She is taking his map and compass because she needs them, and being brisk about it so she does not have to think about why his tent is empty. Businesslike. Two short lines, both to herself.*

**TRACEY**
> Now, let's get that compass.

> (Decision made. This is the closest she gets all morning to competent.)

**TRACEY**
> Right. East. Let's go east.

## Scene 05 — The Forest Road To The Glade

*The forest road, walking east, barefoot. Something crosses the path ahead of her — a wolf, at distance, which ignores her completely. The fear here is new and she has no vocabulary for it yet. These are three small sounds, not speeches.*

> (Involuntary. She has stopped walking before she has decided to.)

**TRACEY**  *(A muffled gasp)*
> Ah—!

**TRACEY**
> What's happening?

> (Not a curse. Closer to a prayer she does not believe in.)

**TRACEY**
> Jesus!

*The Glade, where her friends were supposed to be. Their belongings are scattered, the bonfire is long dead, the sleeping bags are empty. This is where the level turns and where the actor's job changes. Tracey's defences are verbal, and here they stop working. She calls out, gets nothing, and keeps calling anyway.*

> (She already knows. She calls out anyway — that is the tragedy of the line.)

**TRACEY**  *(Her voice begins to break)*
> Friends?

> (Rylee's name is the one that comes out first. Do not decide why.)

**TRACEY**
> Rylee...

**TRACEY**
> Guys...

> (This should be unfinished. She does not know how the sentence ends.)

**TRACEY**
> I...

**TRACEY**
> Huh?

**TRACEY**
> Who... are...

> (Genuine terror, and the only scream in the level. Do not save anything.)

**TRACEY**  *(fear)*
> Ahhhhh!

**TRACEY**
> I need to get out of here!
